FTL Me

  1. Sign in to fencingtimelive.com and open the tournament schedule or your event.
  2. Tap the address bar, type ftlme, and tap the suggestion with the star.
  3. Leave the tab open. It refreshes every 30 s; Refresh updates at once.
  4. Pool details and Full tableau open in a new tab. If the panel is gone, run ftlme again.
  5. change tracks another fencer.
